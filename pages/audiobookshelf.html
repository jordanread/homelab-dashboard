Audiobookshelf

Self-hosted audiobook and podcast server with cross-device progress sync — no Audible, no subscriptions.

↗ Open Audiobookshelf

What it does

Audiobookshelf is a full-featured self-hosted server for your audiobook and podcast collection. It streams audio directly from the Pi, tracks listening progress across devices, syncs bookmarks and chapter positions, and supports multiple users — all on your local network (and via Tailscale remotely).

Getting started

  1. Open abs.lan in a browser or the mobile app.
  2. Log in with your credentials (ask Jordan if you haven't got them).
  3. Browse your library — audiobooks are organized by author and series.
  4. Tap any book to start streaming; progress is saved automatically.
📱 Mobile app: Install the free Audiobookshelf app (iOS/Android). In Settings, set the server URL to http://abs.lan (on home Wi-Fi) or the Tailscale IP when away. Log in and your progress follows you everywhere.

Adding books

Drop audiobook folders into the library path on the Pi. ABS will auto-scan and import them. Supported formats include M4B, MP3, FLAC, OGG, and more. Metadata is fetched automatically from Audnexus/OpenLibrary.

Library path: /mnt/data/audiobooks/ Format: Author Name/Book Title/files.mp3

Podcasts

ABS can also subscribe to podcast RSS feeds and automatically download new episodes. Great for archiving shows you want to keep without relying on any streaming service.
