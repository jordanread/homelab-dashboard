Nextcloud

Your de-Googled cloud. Files, calendar, contacts, and more — entirely self-hosted.

↗ Open Nextcloud

What it does

Nextcloud is the Swiss Army knife of self-hosting. It replaces Google Drive (file sync), Google Calendar, Google Contacts, and Google Photos — all from your Pi. Files sync across devices via the desktop and mobile apps, calendars and contacts sync via CalDAV/CardDAV, and the web interface provides a full file manager.

Mobile setup — Files

  1. Install the Nextcloud app (iOS/Android).
  2. Server URL: http://cloud.lan
  3. Log in with your credentials.
  4. Enable Auto Upload in the app to automatically back up photos (though Immich does this better).

Calendar & Contacts sync

Nextcloud supports CalDAV and CardDAV, which means your phone's native calendar and contacts apps can sync to it directly.

# CalDAV (Calendar) Server: http://cloud.lan/remote.php/dav # CardDAV (Contacts) Server: http://cloud.lan/remote.php/dav
iOS: Settings → Mail → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add CalDAV Account. Use the URL above and your Nextcloud credentials.

Nextcloud apps to enable

  • Calendar — Full calendar app in the browser.
  • Contacts — Address book management.
  • Tasks — To-do lists that sync via CalDAV.
  • Notes — Simple markdown notes (lighter than Joplin).
  • Talk — Private chat and video calls on-network.
