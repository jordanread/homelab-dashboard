Immich

High-performance self-hosted photo and video backup with face recognition, map view, and mobile apps.

↗ Open Immich

What it does

Immich is your private Google Photos replacement. The mobile app automatically backs up every photo and video on your phone to the Pi. From there, you get face recognition, location map view, smart albums, sharing, and a beautiful timeline UI — all without a single photo leaving your network.

Setting up the mobile app

  1. Install Immich from your app store.
  2. Server URL: http://photos.lan (on home Wi-Fi) or Tailscale IP when away.
  3. Log in with your credentials.
  4. Go to Settings → Background backup and enable it.
  5. Your photos will start uploading immediately. First backup may take a while!
Tip for the Admiral: Enable Backup over mobile data in the app settings if you want photos backed up even when you're not on Wi-Fi (uses mobile data). Otherwise backup happens automatically on home Wi-Fi.

Shared albums

Both Jordan and the Admiral can create shared albums visible to the other user. Perfect for trip collections — create an album for "Shropshire 2026" and add to it from either phone.

Memory Lane

Immich surfaces "On This Day" memories from previous years — a lovely feature for full-time travelers with years of photos from around the world.

Storage

Photos stored at: /mnt/data/immich/ Thumbnails: /mnt/data/immich/thumbs/ Encoded video: /mnt/data/immich/encoded-video/
